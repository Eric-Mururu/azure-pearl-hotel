# Azure Pearl Hotel & Restaurant

A multi-page, responsive hotel and restaurant website for a Watamu, Kenya concept. This project was customised from a template as part of my web development training.

**Live website:** https://eric-mururu.github.io/azure-pearl-hotel/

## About this project

This site is built on a third-party template (credited in the page footer). I customised it for a coastal hospitality business by replacing the content, menu, prices, room and service information, and contact details.

**Note:** The team members, guest reviews and some figures on the site are sample content for demonstration. They do not represent real people or reviews.

## Pages

- Home
- About Us
- Our Services
- Restaurant Menu (breakfast, lunch and dinner tabs with Ksh prices)
- Book a Room
- Table reservation form
- Our Team
- Guest Reviews
- Contact Us
